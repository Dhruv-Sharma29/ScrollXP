## Short description

## Full description

Turn your screen time into progress with ScrollXP. Build a cozy island, discover cosmetic treasures, and find a little balance in your day.

YOUR ISLAND STARTS HERE
Your cottage and trees are free from the start. Open a welcome chest, place your first treasure, and follow an optional getting-started guide at your own pace.

APP TIME BECOMES CAPPED PROGRESS
Choose up to three installed apps. After you grant Android Usage Access, ScrollXP estimates their whole-app foreground time. Earn 2 XP per eligible minute, up to 120 usage XP per day. It measures app time, not individual Reels, Shorts, TikToks, or scrolling gestures.

BALANCE EARNS MORE
Choose a daily screen-time budget. Complete a full eligible day within that budget to earn a 100-XP bonus. Five completed budgets in one calendar week earn a cosmetic chest; those days do not need to be consecutive. Setup days and days with incomplete or paused tracking do not receive budget bonuses.

A WORLD THAT GROWS
Collect 20 island items, customize your cottage, and choose which owned treasures to display. Preview Seaside cove and Cloud sanctuary, then visit when you reach 5,000 and 10,000 lifetime XP. Your XP is never spent, and unlocked destinations stay yours.

COMFORT AT YOUR PACE
Choose warm Daylight, softer Dusk, or your phone's theme. Reduce motion and turn haptics off if preferred. Optional silent reminders support your chosen time and quiet hours; Android may delay background work. Your world has no daily check-in requirement and never decays.

POSTCARDS FROM YOUR WORLD
Preview and share or save an island PNG. Level and XP are optional and excluded by default. App names and usage history are not included in postcards.

LOCAL BY DEFAULT
Use the island without an account. Daily app-usage totals remain in app-private storage. Android's automatic cloud and device-transfer backups are disabled. Delete local records from Settings at any time. There are no ads, analytics, or AI services.

YOUR ISLAND AT A GLANCE
Add the home-screen island widget to keep a small view of your cottage and progress nearby. Tap it to return to ScrollXP. Updates follow Android's background scheduling.

OPTIONAL BACKUP, NO SUBSCRIPTION
Create an email account and verify it to save or restore your island with Firebase. Backups happen only when you confirm them. They include your game and dated XP rewards, while app names and daily usage totals stay on your phone. Weekly widget detail and 30-day local history are included. Cloud features require Internet and available service capacity.

PRIVATE BALANCE CIRCLES
Invite friends to a seven-day challenge: each full budget day after joining earns one point. Up to six members can compare chosen names and manually shared counts. Join up to three circles. Scores are self-reported; there are no cash prizes or extra XP. Keep invitation codes private.

OPTIONAL SCROLLXP PRO
When available in Google Play, Pro adds Ocean, Rose and Lavender themes and a 90-day local journal. It is a monthly subscription that renews automatically until cancelled in Google Play. The localized price appears before checkout. Widgets, 30-day history, backups and circles stay free. Deleting a ScrollXP account does not cancel a Play subscription.

ScrollXP needs Android 8.0 or later. Tracking and reminder delivery can vary with Android settings and device battery restrictions. XP and cosmetic treasures have no cash value.
