Delete your ScrollXP account

Account-enabled builds offer deletion inside the app. You can also contact the developer if you no longer have the app.

In the app

Open Settings → Explore connected features → Your account → Delete online account. Read the confirmation and enter your account password to verify ownership.

Without the app

Email dev.dhruvsharma29@gmail.com from the address registered to your ScrollXP account, with the subject “ScrollXP account deletion”. We may ask you to verify account ownership. Do not send a password, purchase token, or usage history.

What is deleted

Your friend memberships, shared names and scores, and latest island backup are removed before the Firebase login is deleted. The last member leaving also deletes the circle. If interrupted, reconnect and repeat Delete online account to finish; some memberships may already have been removed. A minimal deletion guard containing only the former account ID and deletion timestamp is retained indefinitely to block old sessions from recreating backups or memberships. It contains no email, password, island or usage records.

Separate controls

Your local island remains on your phone. Use Settings → Delete data and reset island to remove local records. Use Leave circle to remove only that membership. To cancel Pro, open Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions, or manage your Play subscription. Account deletion, reset and uninstalling do not cancel subscriptions or erase Google's transaction records. Saved/shared postcards, recipients' copies of invitations or scores, and Android’s own usage logs remain outside these controls.
