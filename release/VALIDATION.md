Pro is implemented with Play Billing 9.1.0, product `scrollxp_pro`, regular monthly auto-renewing base plan `monthly`. Receipt signatures are checked locally, completed purchases acknowledged and suspended purchases excluded. Existing purchases block a second checkout. Three optional palettes and a 90-day local journal use local entitlement only. Receipts/tokens are not stored in the island database or Firestore. This is not independent server verification or a cloud entitlement; renewal/refund notifications and backend anti-fraud are absent.

**Checkout is disabled in both current APKs**, because the public Play RSA licensing key and Play product setup are not supplied. No fake Pro access, hard-coded price or simulated payment is shipped. Configure and validate through Play Console before accepting payments; see `PLAY_BILLING_SETUP.md`.

Circles use direct client Firestore transactions, verified email accounts, six members, three circles, seven-day rounds and a 48-hour final-score window. Uploaded counts are manually confirmed, self-reported completed budgets; no app names, minutes, daily goal dates, cash prizes or extra XP. Codes are bearer invitations: verified holders can read metadata and join while space is available. Roster reads need membership. There are no continuous listeners, global rankings, chat, server anti-cheat or automatic ended-group cleanup.

Account deletion removes memberships before atomically deleting backup/index and creating the immutable former-UID/timestamp guard, then deleting Auth. Interrupted cleanup may have already removed some memberships and can be retried. Local reset clears game/reminder/export/Pro-theme preferences but leaves online data and Google Play subscriptions. Cancel subscriptions separately in Play. Circle titles persist while other members remain; the last member removes the group. These boundaries are disclosed in the policy and deletion page.
